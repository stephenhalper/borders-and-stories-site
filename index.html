What's inside

One map. Every kind of trip.

Not just a checklist: a real record of how you travel, built from the ground up around the way trips actually happen.

Every border, tracked

Tap a country to mark it visited, then drill into the cities (and in 149 countries, the actual states, provinces, or departments) you've actually been to. Tag how you crossed, by air, sea, or land, and watch the map fill in as you go.

Flights, reimagined

Log every flight and watch your own rotating globe of routes take shape: total miles flown, longest flight, and how many times around the Earth you've gone.

Memories that stick

Attach photos and journal entries to any country, city, or flight: a living scrapbook tied to the map, not buried in a camera roll.

Real routes

Import a GPX file from a trail or road trip and see the exact path overlaid on the map: not just a pin, the actual route. Export and share any route as a GPX file whenever you want.

Landmarks & wonders

Chase down UNESCO World Heritage Sites (from the Pyramids to Machu Picchu) and check them off as you go.

Lists & favorites

Rank your own top-5 lists and mark a favorite city, food, or restaurant in every country: the details that make a trip yours.

Budget, without the math

Log what you spend in local currency and see it converted to yours automatically, with predicted vs. actual cost for every trip.

Oceans & seas

Check off the world's oceans and seas as you touch them: swam in, sailed through, or just dipped a toe in. If you got wet, it counts.

Why Borders & Stories

You’ve been places. Probably a lot of places.

Borders & Stories gives all those countries, flights, road trips, questionable travel decisions, favorite cities, and “wait, what year were we there?” memories somewhere to live. It’s a personal travel tracker, world travel map, travel journal, and trip budget tracker, built around your actual travel history. Most travel trackers stop at “countries visited.” This one keeps going: cities, regions, flights, routes, budgets, and the stories behind them, all tied to one map.

Buy it once. That’s it.

Borders & Stories is a one-time purchase, not the start of a subscription. No ads, no premium tier holding features behind another payment, no paying again to unlock more of the app you already bought. Buy the app and use the app.

No background location tracking

Borders & Stories doesn’t continuously track your location. Your travel history is built from the places, flights, expenses, stories, photos, and GPX routes you choose to add.
